# Twelfth AI

> Twelfth is a commercial workspace for retail and category teams. It turns pricing, inventory, range, promotion and supplier signals into reviewed decisions, accountable action and a shared record of why the team acted.

Twelfth AI Pty Ltd (ACN 695 769 138) operates twelfth.ai, the Twelfth software platform and the documentation at twelfth.ai/docs/. Public product information is available without authentication. Workspace data requires an authorised user: a signed-in member, an assistant a member has connected over OAuth, or a workspace-scoped MCP bearer key.

## When to use Twelfth

- Use Twelfth when a retail or category team needs to identify which commercial issues deserve attention, understand the evidence behind them, review a recommendation or keep the resulting decision and owner attached.
- Use the Twelfth MCP server when an authorised user asks an agent about their workspace identity, operating defaults, members, roles or open actions.
- Use the role and industry pages when deciding whether Twelfth fits a buyer, category manager, merchandise planner, category leader, merch analyst or a supported retail trade.
- Do not use Twelfth MCP for anonymous research, to make changes, or to act without the user’s authorisation. The current MCP tools are read-only.

## Retail category management glossary

The full text of every term page and calculator, in one file: [llms-full.txt](https://twelfth.ai/llms-full.txt). Each page is also served as Markdown at its URL plus `.md`.

- [Category management](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/category-management): Category management is running a group of related products as one business, with one owner accountable for its range, pricing, promotions and suppliers.
- [Category role](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/category-role): A category role is the job a category does for the retailer, such as driving trips, earning margin or filling the basket. The role sets how it is judged.
- [Assortment](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/assortment): An assortment is the set of products a retailer chooses to carry in a category, for the whole chain, a cluster of stores or a single channel.
- [Range review](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/range-review): A range review is the periodic decision about which lines to add, keep or delist in a category, typically made once or twice a year.
- [Category captain](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/category-captain): A category captain is the supplier a retailer relies on for advice about a whole category, including its range, shelf layout and promotional calendar.
- [Cannibalisation](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/cannibalisation): Cannibalisation is when a new or promoted product takes its sales from products the retailer already sells, so the category total barely grows.
- [Own label](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/own-label): Own label is product a retailer brands and sells itself, also called private label or a house brand, and usually made for it by a contract manufacturer.
- [GMROI](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/gmroi): GMROI (gross margin return on inventory investment) is the gross profit a product earns for every dollar of inventory held, measured at cost.
- [Price index](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/price-index): A price index compares your price for a product with a competitor's price for the same product as one number, where a set value (often 100) is parity.
- [Price position](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/price-position): Price position is where your price sits against the market for a comparable product at a point in time: above, level with or below your competitors.
- [Matched SKU](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/matched-sku): A matched SKU is a competitor's listing confirmed to be the same product as one of yours, so the two prices can be compared fairly.
- [Price intelligence](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/price-intelligence): Price intelligence is collecting competitors' published prices for products you sell, matching them to your own lines, and acting on the gaps that matter.
- [Markdown](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/markdown): A markdown is a permanent price reduction on a product, usually to sell stock that is not moving at its original price.
- [Clearance](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/clearance): Clearance is the final markdown that exits a product's remaining stock, at whatever price frees the stock and the space it occupies.
- [Promotional incrementality](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/promotional-incrementality): Promotional incrementality is the share of sales during a promotion that would not have happened without it.
- [Gross-profit floor](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/gp-floor): A gross-profit floor is the lowest margin percentage a category accepts on a line before a more senior person has to agree to the decision.
- [Sell-through](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/sell-through): Sell-through is the share of available stock that sold in a period: units sold divided by units available, usually tracked weekly or over a season.
- [Weeks of cover](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/weeks-of-cover): Weeks of cover is how long current stock will last at the expected rate of sale: stock on hand divided by forecast weekly sales.
- [Stockout](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/stockout): A stockout is when a product a shopper wants is not available to buy, whether on the shelf, in the warehouse or online.
- [Lost demand](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/lost-demand): Lost demand is sales that did not happen because a product was unavailable, hard to find or priced out, so they never appear in a sales report.
- [Replenishment](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/replenishment): Retail replenishment replaces sold stock in time for the next sale, through a shelf refill, a transfer or a new supplier order.
- [Stock transfer](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/stock-transfer): A stock transfer moves inventory a retailer already holds from one location to another, such as between stores or from a warehouse to a store.
- [Demand forecast](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/demand-forecast): A demand forecast is the expected future sales of a product, usually per location and per week, used to plan buys, stock and space.
- [Remit](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/remit): A remit is the part of the range a buyer or category manager owns and decides for: the categories, suppliers or products they are accountable for.
- [Guardrail](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/guardrail): In twelfth, a guardrail is a commercial limit every recommendation passes through, such as a margin floor, an approval limit or a locked supplier setting.
- [Authority limit](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/authority-limit): An authority limit is the largest value a person can commit without escalation; above it, a decision waits for sign-off from someone more senior.
- [Tracked set](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/tracked-set): In twelfth, a tracked set is the group of products a workspace has asked twelfth to watch competitor prices for, and the evidence table behind them.
- [Decision pipeline](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/decision-pipeline): In twelfth, the decision pipeline is the path commercial work takes: surfaced into Triage, settled into Pending, then recorded as a decision.
- [Decision record](https://twelfth.ai/glossary/decision-record): In twelfth, a decision record is the permanent, timestamped entry made when a decision is committed, holding what was committed and what was recommended.
